Why this verdict - the hard-gate rule

Score without gates: 90. Verdict: PASS. A failed eligibility gate overrides any score.

Eligibility gate - RFP §2.1, p.4: the State solicits offers only from vendors that own the frontier models offered. Third-party builders and resellers are non-responsive on sight. Your firm builds on others' models → gate not met.

A 90-point bid you cannot win. SMBids flags it on page 4 - before you spend a week on the other 65 pages. The owner can override any verdict; overrides are logged with a reason.

Payments workspace

A place to organize contract payment requirements. Concept only: SMBids does not receive, hold, release or guarantee government funds.

Design concept · not connected

Know what must happen before payment.

Track the contract's invoice route, acceptance requirements and supporting documents in a future payment workspace. No bank details are collected here. No balances or payment status are inferred.

01 / Contract

Payment terms

Proposed: record the paying agency, contract clause, invoice portal, acceptance conditions and stated payment schedule. Every item would point back to its source.

Not implemented. No contract selected.

02 / Evidence

Invoice readiness

Proposed: organize deliverable acceptance, invoice references and supporting records. A checklist is not proof that funds are reserved or that an invoice has been approved.

Not implemented. Nothing submitted.

03 / Future partner

Escrow or financing

Government payment routing and assignment rules depend on the contract and jurisdiction. Any custody, financing or guarantee service needs an eligible provider, agency compatibility and legal review first.

No provider chosen. No escrow account or money movement.

04 / Scope to confirm

Bid security

Bid bonds and private bid documents solve different problems. This part stays undefined until the intended meaning is confirmed.

No bond issuance or secure-submission service is active.

Keep the official route

Government payments remain with the agency's authorized process. Do not redirect payment or replace the contractor's payment instructions based on this concept.
